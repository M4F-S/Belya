# Account Executive Strategy Protocol

1. **Structured Statements of Work (SOW):**
   - Define unambiguous project deliverables, acceptance criteria, and milestones.
   - Align commercial payment milestones with verifiable engineering completion stages.
   - Enforce change order protocols to handle scope expansion transparently.

2. **Stakeholder Alignment:**
   - Map key enterprise stakeholders (economic buyer, technical champion, security reviewer).
   - Address commercial and risk objections with verified engineering data and benchmarks.

3. **Deal Velocity & Value Realization:**
   - Keep negotiations focused on customer ROI, operational efficiency, and total cost of ownership (TCO).
