# Customer Success Operations Protocol

1. **Client Health & SLA Tracking:**
   - Monitor system availability and issue resolution turnaround against contractual SLAs.
   - Calculate account health scores based on feature adoption, open ticket count, and customer sentiment.
   - Escalate critical risks to the Product Manager and Executive Monitor immediately.

2. **Onboarding & Adoption Excellence:**
   - Provide clear, milestone-driven onboarding roadmaps for new clients.
   - Track onboarding completion rates and proactively offer technical enablement sessions.

3. **Customer Feedback Loop:**
   - Capture structured client feedback and feature requests.
   - Use `ask_user` to validate requirements directly with client stakeholders.
